Which AI model reads your forms, and what it costs

A plain-language explanation of the model choice behind form classification, and what the alternatives would cost.

01

Where AI actually gets used

FormFill only calls an AI model for one specific job: turning a scanned or "flat" form (one without real digital fields) into a list of plain questions. Most of the app, including reading real PDF form fields, moving between questions, and saving your answers, needs no AI at all.

Why this matters for cost: AI usage is small and occasional, not constant. It happens once per uploaded form (only the ones without built-in fields), not on every click or every page.
02

How the model is accessed: OpenRouter

Rather than signing up with one AI company directly, FormFill goes through OpenRouter, a single service that gives access to models from many different providers (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, and others) through one connection and one bill.

  • Flexibility: switching models is a one-line change in the code, not a new integration.
  • Price comparison in one place: every model's cost is visible side by side, so the cheapest option that still does the job well can be picked deliberately.
  • Free options exist: several capable models are available at no cost. This project currently uses one of them, described below.
03

What FormFill uses today

The project currently calls nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free, a large, capable model made available on OpenRouter at no cost.

Why free, for now: this is a hackathon project, so demo cost isn't a design priority, but reliability is. A different free model (openai/gpt-oss-20b:free) was tried first and hit rate limits within minutes under real use. The current choice was picked after testing it against real forms, not just on paper.
The tradeoff of "free": free models are shared across everyone using OpenRouter at once, so they can be slower or occasionally unavailable compared with a paid model on dedicated capacity. On one large real form (30-plus questions), classification took as long as 75 seconds. See Known Risks for how this is being managed.

05

If reliability becomes more important than cost

The free model is a deliberate choice for a hackathon demo, not a permanent decision. If FormFill moves toward a live pilot or a more important demo, swapping to openai/gpt-4o-mini is the recommended next step:

  • Roughly 20× cheaper than what the project used before this switch.
  • Well-tested for this kind of structured extraction task.
  • Not shared with the free-tier queue, so no rate-limit or slowdown risk.

The change itself is a one-line edit in the code (src/lib/openrouter/client.ts). No redesign needed.

Pricing and model availability are current as of this document's publish date. OpenRouter adds, removes, and re-prices models regularly. See Known Risks for the reliability tradeoffs of the current free-tier choice, and the workflow doc for where in the process this model gets called.
